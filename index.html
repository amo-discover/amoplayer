Your videos, links and media servers

Amo Player is a media player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. It plays your own videos, video links, M3U playlists and media servers.

Choose a file

Videos and M3U playlists on your device, an SD card or a USB drive, with subtitle files next to a video picked up automatically.

Video or M3U link

Paste a link to a video, a live stream or an M3U playlist. Playlists open with categories and a filter.

Media server

Type the address of your media server or media web app. Amo Player remembers it and can open it every time it starts.

On every screen

  • Phones, tablets and foldables, with picture in picture.
  • Android TV, driven entirely by the remote.
  • MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, TS, HLS and DASH; audio tracks, subtitles, quality and speed.
  • Fifteen languages.

No content included

Amo Player does not provide, sell or preload any films, channels or playlists. You play media you own or have the right to watch.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you add stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
