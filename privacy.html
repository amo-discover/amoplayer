Privacy policy

Updated 28 October 2026 (donations added). Applies to Amo Player for Android phones and tablets and Amo Player for Android TV.

In short

Amo Player has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software that collects data. The developer never receives anything about what you watch. The only information the developer receives is a donation record, and only if you choose to donate: the email address you enter, the amount and the date (see Donations). Nothing is sold or shared.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps these items in its own private storage on your device only:

  • the links, playlists, folders and media server addresses you added or opened recently;
  • where you stopped in each video, so it can continue from there;
  • your settings: language, audio, subtitle and quality choices, picture fit and speed;
  • cookies and site data of the media servers and web apps you open in the app, kept like a web browser keeps them.

You can remove recent items, playlists, servers and folders in the app, and everything at once by clearing the app's storage in the system settings or by uninstalling the app. The app has backups turned off, so this data is not copied to any cloud backup.

Network connections

Amo Player connects to the addresses you enter or open: video links, playlists, media servers and web apps. Like any player or browser, it then sends those services the usual technical information of an internet request, such as your IP address, the app's user agent and the requested address. Those services are run by their owners and their own privacy policies apply. The app also connects to Google Play for donations, and to the developer's donation server only on the Donate screen and to finish a donation you made. It makes no other requests: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or update checks.

Donations

Donating is optional. Payment is handled by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer never sees your card or payment details. When you donate, the app sends the developer's donation server the email address you typed and Google Play's reference of the purchase. The server asks Google Play to confirm the purchase and stores:

  • your email address;
  • the donation amount, and the price and currency Google Play charged;
  • the billing country Google Play reports;
  • the date of the donation and whether it was refunded.

This record is used only to keep an accurate list of donations. It is not sold, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising or tracking. Your IP address is used by that server only to limit abuse and is deleted within a day. The record is kept for the developer's accounts. You can ask the developer to erase your email address from it at any time (see Contact).

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to play the links, playlists and servers you choose.
  • Keep awake: so the screen and Wi-Fi do not sleep during playback.
  • Google Play billing: for donations.
  • Phones and tablets: no storage or media permission. The app reads only the files and folders you pick in Android's own file picker.
  • Android TV: read videos and audio (storage on Android 12 and older), asked only when you open Files, to list and play the videos and playlists on the TV or a USB drive. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age set by your country's law).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about privacy, or to have your email erased from a donation record, write to the developer email shown on the Amo Player page of the store where you got the app, or open an issue at github.com/amo-discover/amoplayer/issues.
